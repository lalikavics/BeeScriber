WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
The installer copies the program (with its own Python - you do not need to install anything else). At the first start BeeScriber downloads the remaining components once, with one progress bar: yt-dlp, FFmpeg, Deno, the Whisper large-v3-turbo model (1.6 GB) and, with an NVIDIA graphics card, the CUDA libraries (about 1.3 GB).
